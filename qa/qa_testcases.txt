RATE LIMIT VALIDATION
================================================================================

  Endpoint             Rate Limit    Type
  ------------------   ----------    -------
  POST /auth/login     10 req/min    loginRate  (sliding window Redis)
  POST /auth/register  10 req/min    loginRate  (same bucket as login)
  POST /auth/refresh   120 req/min   authRate
  Public endpoint      60 req/min    publicRate
  Auth'd endpoint      120 req/min   authRate

  The script counts loginRate calls via a file counter (RATE_FILE).
  Auto-waits 65 seconds when the counter approaches 9 (margin of 1 from the limit of 10).
